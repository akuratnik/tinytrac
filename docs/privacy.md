# tinytrac privacy policy

tinytrac stores your time entries on your own devices.

If you turn on Google Drive sync, tinytrac stores sync files in its own hidden app folder in your
Google Drive, which only tinytrac can access. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. Your
Google sign-in is kept in your device's keychain.

The developer never receives, sees or stores your data, and tinytrac uses no analytics or
third-party services.

You can remove tinytrac's access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party
connections. Signing out in tinytrac keeps your local data.

Questions: [tinytrac@expertstudio.net](mailto:tinytrac@expertstudio.net)
